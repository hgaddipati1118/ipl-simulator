## Caveats

- Fielding is catches per chance the side created, plus run-out involvement. Position is not observed: fine-leg / third-man bowlers and slip fielders are not separated (bowlers are compared with bowlers and half of the class gap is kept), so a rating mixes skill with where a player is usually posted.
- Byes are the best-measured keeping component, but how they are scored differs by competition (event-season factors absorb most of it); a keeper with a short career in one league with unusually few recorded byes (e.g. Nurul Hasan) can rate higher than his reputation. Stumpings and catches behind are mostly bowler-driven; their split-half reliability is low, so they move a keeper's rating little.
- Players with a thin record sit near 50 (shrinkage); only players with 100+ innings are listed above.
